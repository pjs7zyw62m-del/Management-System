2026MARINE CORPSIN TESTING

Next-Generation MCCUU

Lightweight material, revised sizing, two cuts, and an added crotch gusset aim to improve comfort, durability, and movement. In August 2026 the Marine Corps began limited wear evaluations at Parris Island; it has not been described as a completed fleet-wide rollout.

2025JOINT FORCESFIELDING

Two-Piece Chemical Protection

The UIPE FoS Air two-piece undergarment is being fielded to some aircrews. It is designed to provide chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear protection beneath the familiar flight suit with less thermal and movement burden than older outer suits.

2025–2026ARMYMODULAR DESIGN

Modular Scalable Vest

The Army’s scalable vest system supports different protective configurations according to mission needs. Its ongoing improvements target reduced weight and continued protection, rather than simply adding more rigid equipment.

2024 FIELDINGARMYHELMET SYSTEM

Next-Generation Combat Helmet

The Army’s NG-IHPS entered unit fielding in February 2024. The helmet is designed to integrate communications, night-vision mounts, and additional protection while reducing the weight needed to achieve comparable threat protection.
