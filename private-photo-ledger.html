TWO-PERSON ACCESS

How this works

01

The chosen employee uploads a private photograph. It is not published to the Network feed or Military Library.

02

The BLACKCELL owner accepts the photo for any amount, including $0.00, or rejects it. The decision is saved and cannot be undone.

03

Both sides see the verified balance. New manual rejections reduce the bill by $0.35 each, and confirmed permanent deletions by $1.00 each. These are ledger credits, not bank refunds.

This is a private record only. BLACKCELL does not transfer money or automatically make payments here.
